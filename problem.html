What is the Problem?

Texas is losing its green canopy at an alarming rate. Here are the three main issues we are facing right now:

1. Rapid Urbanization

As cities like Dallas, Austin, and Houston grow, thousands of acres of native forests are being cleared to make room for parking lots and buildings. This creates "Heat Islands" where city temperatures rise dangerously high.

2. Extreme Droughts

Texas weather is getting tougher. Long periods without rain put massive stress on our older trees, making them weak and more likely to fall during storms.

3. Invasive Species

Bugs and plants that don't belong in Texas are moving in and "choking out" our local trees, preventing new saplings from growing naturally.

Without our trees, Texas loses its shade, its clean air, and its natural beauty.

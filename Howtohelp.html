What the Organization is doing

Tree Planting Initiatives

We are currently planting over 500 native trees across Texas every month to combat heat islands in our cities.

Educational Outreach

Our team visits local schools to teach students about the importance of our state's ecosystem and how to care for young saplings.

Water Conservation

We are developing new irrigation methods that use 30% less water while still keeping our urban forests healthy and green.

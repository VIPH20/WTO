Information

The Texas Trees Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating a healthier and greener Texas through urban forestry and education. They focus on cooling down "heat islands" in major cities by planting thousands of native trees to provide shade and cleaner air. One of their biggest projects is the Cool Schools program, which transforms hot, concrete playgrounds into lush, outdoor learning spaces for students. They also manage a large tree farm where they grow local species specifically chosen to survive the tough Texas climate. By using scientific data and community volunteers, the foundation works to ensure that the state’s urban canopy is protected for future generations.

The foundation also uses advanced satellite mapping technology to identify the hottest neighborhoods in Texas, ensuring they plant trees exactly where the shade is needed most.
